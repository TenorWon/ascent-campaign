### Arrival

In 5206 PET, an enormous, interstellar vessel touched down just outside of the [[Hult]] providence on [[Alagrii]]. The ship appeared to be running at minimal power, adorned with solar plates as potentially its only source of electricity. An [[Alagriian]] security patrol stationed above orbit had attempted to make contact, but no one inside of the strange ship spoke a word of even [[Universal]], a language specifically designed for all people in the galaxy to comprehend one another. When the visitors finally emerged from their transport on solid ground, it was clear why.

They called themselves Humans. As royal forces finally came to aid the Alagriian government, translators began to decipher vital information. Many of the strange visitors were dying, all while their scientists had sealed themselves off to desperately continue working on a way to save them. Doctors all the way from [[Mesokarr]] and [[Gilomar]] began to triage the Humans as they died in hundreds. Some tens of thousands had arrived on the titanic ship, and the navigator had managed to explain that four others may be on the way, if those onboard were still alive.

Three more ships appeared at the site on Alagrii in the next few days, and before long, the Kingdom of the Sun had created and began administering vaccines for the illness the Humans had brought with them. Nearly 200,000 people now found themselves displaced from their home, which historians continue to struggle to find information on. The "arks," as they were called, were made primarily for transporting the last of a dying species in massive number. They lacked sophisticated technology like flight logs, cosmic mapping, etc. It also seemed that the Humans were technologically archaic compared to nearly every sentient species in the Larentum galaxy.

For the next six centuries, Humans have integrated at varying rates into galactic society. Some still remember what their home solar system was like, while many grow up never hearing the names of planets like [[Earth]] or [[Mars]].

### Culture

There is no purely Human culture anymore. Memories of a far-off galaxy have been passed down in traditions and family names over the generations, but it is conceivable to believe that much has been lost to time. Some professors of culture have tried to preserve accounts and records from the original refugees, but such knowledge is scarce. For most Humans today, they likely identify more closely with the culture of the planet they grew up on rather than that of their species.

Humans can be found in many places. According to their migratory patterns following the Kingdom of the Sun's species preservation initiative, they spread slowly out from Alagrii before a great many picked up and headed to Mesokarr. Once they were connected to the main hub of the Kingdom, Humans began to look beyond the nation they landed in. Human communities began to form interspersed among the neutral bodies and the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]]'s outskirts. Once they found Abrilos, they began to flock to it as it showed off an even more unfamiliar and promising side of the galaxy than the Human population had yet to see.

In the present day, Humans live in a variety of states throughout the galaxy. Regardless of whether or not their ancestors were able to integrate, many today have found some level of success, be that work or family. However there are still many who suffer economically, even to the point of homelessness. The Galactic Union has resorted to zoning large plots of tent-crowded land for communities of such people who have yet to integrate. Scholars are yet to land on a solution, but some hypothesize that it is due to the extended travel of those Humans who sought to see the cities of the Union for themselves.

### Biology

Human biology is rather simple compared to that of other sentient species. One heart, one brain, one stomach, two lungs, and so on. While anatomical similarities can certainly be drawn between Humans and certain other species, evolutionary scientists theorize that such similarities only continue up to a certain point as a result of humanity's extragalactic origins.

Very few of the individuals first to arrive in Larentum were free of illness, thus little is known if said condition had any effect on their anatomy. The virus, dubbed "Nox Aeternum" by the ark scientists, was itself made extinct just under a year after the Humans' arrival.
