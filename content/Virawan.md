### Culture

One of the founding societies of the [[The Kingdom of the Sun|Kingdom of the Sun]] along with the [[Mesokarran|Mesokarrans]], the Virawan are known as creators. In older times, before [[Larentum#Galactization|galactization]], they practiced a great many different crafts. However once the Kingdom formed relations with planets like [[Alagrii]] which were rich in valuable minerals, many artisans began to practice the art of smithing. For centuries since, the Virawan have been masters of metal and crystal working, able to make anything from custom tech to high-grade weaponry.

When the [[Gifted]] first appeared, it is said that the discovery of how to control one's abilities was first made on [[Virawa]]. This is thought to be because of the locals' expertise with [[emparite]], which was common on the planet at the time despite not being native to its crust. Thus, the Virawan are credited with understanding over the physical materials that interact with [[Gifts]]. The Kingdom of the Sun will often requisition emparite and [[pallorite]] tools, weapons, and more from a number of forges throughout royal territory in order to keep its Gifted workforce well equipped.

Among their own people and peers, age and experience are highly respected, alongside skill, though it is usually assumed that these qualities go hand in hand. An especially experienced Virawan artisan will grow their hair out to represent their years in their field. For those that do not decide to pursue a craft, they are naturally somewhat on the outside of the social circle. Most Virawan view these individuals as largely incapable or unintelligent. To make technology or to make art is respectable, but to pursue other ventures shows a lack of skill, and thus a lack of motivation to acquire the necessary skills.

### Biology

Virawan are naturally very dexterous, as their extraordinarily long arms and fingers make maneuvering barely a challenge. With how tall they are and how long their limbs are, however, pumping blood is an uphill battle, and thus they evolved a second heart to support the effort. Though they possess fingers on each hand, their feet are unlike most other species, as they have a hoof on each leg. It is theorized that these are remainders of the four-legged species they evolved from, or perhaps that they acquired this trait to navigate rough terrain.
