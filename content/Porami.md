### Culture

When the Porami lost the protection of the [[Final Systems]], they absolutely panicked. Being very small on an extremely hazardous planet full of fog and dangerous predators makes life incredibly difficult, more so when the government that once protected the people from said predators is toppled in a [[The War for Liberation|galactic war]]. That's why they were one of the first to enter into an agreement with the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]], who quickly offered them the safety they so desperately needed.

Despite being at home on [[Poram]], the Porami are in constant danger in many areas of the planet. They may have been the prevailing sentient organism, but they are not top of the Porami food chain. They have built their villages and towns around their history of needing to keep things out, which continues to this day, though the task is easier now with the Union's help. It is a stereotype that most Porami are scared, little people, but in many cases it can be true.

There are few who dare to dream of a life beyond hiding, especially those who are not born under the tradition of social deference that is usually taught in Porami families. Most people are taught to be defensive or to run in situations of conflict, as that is how the species has survived.

### Biology

The Porami are undeniably the smallest sentient species in the galaxy as far as stature. Though they have largely gotten by via their wits rather than their genetic superiority over other creatures on Poram, they do have a few adaptations that have secured their place. Their dark green skin makes for extremely solid camouflage against their green, boggy surroundings. They also have a way of internalizing moisture from the air, which makes them resistant to the heat and humidity of their surroundings.
