The Larentum galaxy is home to countless sentient lifeforms. Despite the civilizations that have taken shape over time within it, it has still not come close to being fully explored.

### Primary Evolutionary-Period

Scholars note that there has never been a point in Larentum's history when one sentient being existed alone. However, it is theorized that one of the oldest—and potentially the first—was the ancestor of the [[Paranui]].

From the first recordings taken from the earliest civilizations, it has been deduced that life began initially on planets orbiting a certain distance away from stars at a particularly young stage in the stellar life cycle; now referred to as suns. This sort of life thrived in abundance throughout the galaxy for millions of years, undisturbed by any off-world interference. During this extremely long period, organisms experienced a scientific phenomenon known as evolution, thus this stretch of 146 million galactic standard years (estimated) being dubbed "Evolutionary Time" or "The Primary Evolutionary Period"

At the end of this time, sentient organisms began to form societal organizations on their planets, working together over hundreds to thousands of years to develop their technology until they were able to expand out into their solar systems. From there, it was only a matter of time until they found each other.

### Galactization

Finding that each solar system, planet, and moon held resources unlike all the others, many people who set out amongst the stars found it best to collaborate. These conditional agreements began as tentative alliances, but evolved over time into coalitions of likeminded governments. Over time, these groups coalesced into governing bodies themselves, until intergalactic interaction was so commonplace that many began to feel as if they could not function without it.

Galactization—as it was called first in the year 2364 PET (Post Evolutionary Time)—is the exchange between peoples and governments of celestial bodies across the Larentum galaxy. The most well known point of exchange is, notably, the [[Auvacian]] faith, but the effects of this phenomenon are still being studied today.
