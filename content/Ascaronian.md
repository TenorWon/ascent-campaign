### Culture

A people largely defined by their imposing presence, theirs is a nation of contradictions. A natural armor allowed them to survive the trials of life before high-tech with great ease, which has led them to become an incredibly social people when unthreatened. Used to being put on the defensive (as evolution did not provide them any natural weapons, save for a bony body,) Ascaronian etiquette in general is a dialogue of deference. Thus, they find it difficult to embrace or even lend support to conflict.

As a still-independent system, the Ascaronians have quite a unique outlook on the galaxy. On one hand, they are constantly in the middle of intergalactic politics, but on the other, there is no need to take sides when you are completely removed. They are by no means isolationists, but they carry with them a generational fear of being mistaken for aggressors. When [[Larentum#Galactization|galactization]] came, other systems were quick to hesitate in dealing with such rugged looking people. Representatives from [[Ascaron]] eventually found that the only way to establish peaceful relations was to submit to the needs of their hosts as quickly as possible and reassure them that they were not a threat.

The repeated practice of having to identify oneself as "not a threat" created a generational cycle over time of inherited self consciousness. In more traditional Ascaronian areas, this can manifest as a tradition of hiding one's physical form altogether and only revealing it to those one is familiar with socially. Most do not practice this, but will still make an effort to ensure another's comfort before their own.

### Biology

Ascaron's evolutionary history is fraught with predators who relied on blunt force to kill prey. To survive, the Ascaronians developed a thick endoskeleton, flexible enough to allow for speed, but dense enough to make them much more difficult to kill. This additional bone density causes their skeleton to protrude or "bump up" under the skin, the visibility of which is seen as a traditional sign of strength and status. More skeletal structure also means more muscles than the average two-legged species would have, giving them a reputation for athleticism that they sometimes attempt to sweep away.
