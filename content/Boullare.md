### Culture

These large, furry people actually had quite a bit of trouble joining the other worlds of the galaxy in exchange and communication. This was largely due to how integral tribalism was to their culture. Before they truly began to [[Larentum#Galactization|galactize]], the Boullare lived in separate but cooperative tribes, commanded by chiefs, in an assortment of nations across [[Boullaris]].

No one is sure specifically why this structure broke down, but the broader reason is clear. The Boullare, once given time to adopt [[Auvacianism]], left their old traditions behind in favor of "the true way." With the help of the [[The Kingdom of the Sun|Kingdom of the Sun]], the nation that adopted Boullaris some years after the fall of the [[Final Systems]], the Boullare adjusted their world to reflect the qualities of the royal capital world, [[Mesokarr]]. Of course, the two are not identical due to geology and the architectural past, but many monuments of the past were torn down to make way for [[sanctums]] and other, more modern additions.

Some do hold fast to the old ways, however. As the majority of Boullare are more than happy to walk away from their tribal history, many elders try their best to teach the youth of today the traditions and history that has been lost to generations of modernization. These people are few and far between—and often shunned by their fellows if they allow their nostalgia to get in the way of what is truly relevant today, especially regarding worship of Auvac.

### Biology

Boullare are somewhat giant, making it clear how they evolved into viable predators with ample survivability. With their sharp claws and teeth, as well as keen eyes, they are able to rip and tear anything that is misfortunate enough to be unprotected. Since they spent such a long time hunting and exploring without clothing or armor, their hide has become thick enough that it still provides resistance to some amount of damage to this day. Their fur serves mainly as a deterrent for the cold, as they are such large people that they burn more energy and lose far more body heat than others. However, this has never been an issue, as their immense strength has allowed them to climb and fell trees to shelter them for as long as history has documented their existence.
