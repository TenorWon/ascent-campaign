### Formation

The largest corporate entity in [[Larentum]] did not start that way. In the [[The Before Times|Before Times]], [[The Weraga Company]] rose to fame for creating small trinkets for the purpose of convenience in everyday life. As Eternus proudly touts, [[Velaios Weraga]] was a proud small business owner who pulled themself out of their old house on [[Ettora]] with a drive to provide for their community. From there, that mission blossomed into everything we know Eternus Galactic to be today.

From household appliances to [[drones]] and everything in between, Eternus has established itself as the go-to option for whatever one needs in this galaxy. Now owned and operated by Head of Functions [[Lim Weraga]] from the company's headquarters in [[Ti Gal]], they work with every system galaxy-wide to better the lives of every person.

### Customs

The Weraga line is one that is extremely flush with credits, though those credits have historically been used for good purposes. During the end of the [[The War for Liberation|War for Liberation]], it was another descendant of Velaios who joined with several other businesses in operation at the time to fund a massive rebellion of [[Velastos]], greatly hastening the fall of the [[Final Systems]].  Currently, a great amount of Eternus' profits go to the [[Union Senate]] for government programs and financial relief.

Overall, Eternus Galactic is rated as one of the best places in the Galactic Union to work, even with competition for employment in general continuing to become steeper all around. Their employees are normally middle to upper-middle class, and tend to live comfortably.

As far as available products, Eternus has billions. Their previous owners have bought into a great many successful industries and companies, and Lim Weraga has picked up right where they left off. There are very few corporations in the galaxy that can even be compared to Eternus in size, and if they can be, they likely have entirely different business models, audiences, or industries.

Above all, Eternus Galactic values convenience for the consumer. They manufacture all of their products themselves, deliver them if they are not easily purchased in store or by independent merchant, and even provide helpful software and applications to go with most of the hardware they have available. You will be hard pressed to find someone with anything bad to say about this gentle giant, especially in this day and age where the simple comfort can seem just a bit more fleeting.
