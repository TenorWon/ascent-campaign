### Formation

In 2371 PET, a prophet named [[Noleis]] gained acclaim within [[The Accord]], the largest multi-system alliance of the time. Not much is known about the mysterious figure, but what is known is that he was the man to bring the flame of [[Auvacianism]] to [[Mesokarr]]. The religion spread like wildfire, and it was only a few standard years before every [[Mesokarran]] was begging their leadership to take to the stars to spread the word of Auvac.

Before long, the Mesokarran royal navy was spread far and wide on behalf of the reigning queen. The religion jumpstarted the effort to [[Larentum#Galactization|galactize]], which led to newly developing relationships with the [[Gilam]], [[Araantem]], and [[Virawa]] systems. These initial outreach efforts eventually led to amicable and mutual exchanges of culture, technology, and resources, which proved too good a situation to leave as a simple temporary alliance. Thus, a long-term treaty was signed on Mesokarr in 2392.

However it was not enough for the Mesokarran monarchy, now transitioned to the title of the [[Sunbound Monarch]], to simply have allies. If their god's light was to shine over all the galaxy, then they would need power over this alliance. A short fifteen years after the first treaty was signed, a proposition was put forth for a second. The proposition requested that the leaders of each of Mesokarr's three closest allies acknowledge the Sunbound Monarch as the sole leader of a new, central, and intergalactic government. The last of the traditional, non-religious governmental houses on the allied planets had no choice but to accept when the conditions of the treaty were made public. Their people had been slowly converting to Auvac worship over time, and it was only a matter of years before those in power adapted or drowned beneath the new wave. Finally, the Kingdom of the Sun was established in 2407.

Today, the Kingdom of the Sun remains the largest organization of civilized governments in the entire galaxy. Their size is rivaled only by the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]], which emerged more than two millennia after its formation. Despite its size, royal influence over the galaxy has been steadily edged out over time as the Union has grown, but the two galactic powers have continued to maintain their amicable understanding and mutual relationship.

### Customs

Royal society revolves almost entirely around the worship of Auvac. There is a [[sanctum]] in nearly every city and town, with the most well-known one being the [[Aureate Sanctum]] in the capital city of [[Vestim]]. People find a way to work attendance to such places every week, if they tie themselves closely with the faith. Clergypeople known as [[oracles]] are responsible for organizing and maintaining the weekly service, otherwise called [[witness]]. Depending on how far up the ladder one goes, there is an intersection between clerical duties and governmental ones. The Kingdom is a theocracy, after all, and therefore relies on oracles in certain positions to advise governing authority figures on what Auvac would want.

No figure is known better for this task than the [[Willbearer]], who assists the Sunbound Monarch in Vestim by giving voice to Auvac Themself. Despite the name of "monarch," both of these devoutly religious positions are chosen by rigorous political testing and ritualistic induction. The Sunbound is mainly labeled as a monarch because of Mesokarr's history as a monarchy.

There has always been a sense of duty to the Kingdom, especially when paired with one's religious duty. This was taken a step further when [[Gifted]] were normalized into society. After the establishment of the first [[academy]] in Vestim, the precedent was set for parents of Gifted children to send them straight to academies for training and honing of their abilities immediately after completion of primary school. Since most royal scholars and clergy agreed that Gifts were abilities given by Auvac Themself, which was quite literally where the name for the condition originated from, there were few people who opposed this mandate. From these academies, the royal workforce was and continues to be strengthened as graduates are drafted into the next step of their life—whether that be as a smelt worker in a [[Virawan]] forge, a healer for a high level hospital, or even a disciple of the royal military.
