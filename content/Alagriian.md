### Culture

The Alagriian people are well known throughout the galaxy for their work ethic. What some may not know, however, is what they do for work. While some ignorant galactic citizens might mistake them for artisans, they are famously a backbone of manual labor, truly competing only with [[drones]] in terms of efficiency. It is not unlikely to find an Alagriian in any job that requires muscle and patience, as they possess these qualities in droves.

What they are most known for, however, is the mining they do. It is the most common occupation on [[Alagrii]], as the planet's crust is rich with valuable resources from lithium to [[emparite]]. The people work tirelessly to remove these ores from the stone so they can be made into resources for the [[The Kingdom of the Sun|Kingdom of the Sun]]. Most of Alagriian culture revolves around this practice, as villages, family traditions, and holidays occur in tight synergy with mining schedules.

This ultimately results in a lower average for education among Alagriians, who may go their whole lives without leaving their home planet. If one does leave their home to pursue success or education out in the galaxy, it is not often that they return. This is thought to be because they come to prefer their new lives elsewhere.

### Biology

Alagrii's surface is nearly all stone, making it extremely difficult to navigate and even more so to live on. The Alagriians' skin tone adjusted to their surroundings to avoid predators, camouflaging themselves with their rocky surroundings. Their skin also developed a thick, calloused texture over time, which helped them to resist the jagged edges they were surrounded by. This in addition to their great strength is also what allowed them to pound into thin stone ground to find where soil was hidden.
