### Culture

[[Ettora]] is a planet widely known for its mystique, even before anyone from off-world got the chance to visit it. That's why it is no surprise that its people are full of whimsy, tricks, and surprises. They may be a neutral people as far as galactic governments go, but their leaders still enjoy making conditional agreements with both the [[The Kingdom of the Sun|Kingdom of the Sun]] and the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]]. Some call it playing both sides, but the Ettoran government is never exactly subtle about what they do politically.

Ettoran people are even more playful than their government is. Known for embracing the galaxy in joy, entertainment, and bliss, they bring the mystique of their culture with them wherever they go. On their home world, this sort of nature is a mandatory part of growing up. There are classes for etiquette, as well as emotion reading, the lessons of which were based on the primary language of the [[Celespran|Celesprans]].

Physicality is also a big part of being in line with Ettoran culture. Thinner is almost always better, a notion which does not exactly fit with the open nature of the rest of the galaxy. There have been independent, health-based investigations into the mental and physical implications of the cultural practices of Ettoran society, but ultimately they remain a neutral nation as well as a valuable ally to both primary galactic powers. As it stands, they demand respect for their traditions, whether they are safe or not.

### Biology

Ettorans can be born at any size according to their genetics, but a combination of dietary restriction and exercise has created a low average weight among the population. Aside from this, the people possess all matter of different skin, eye, and hair colors. The main biological feature they have all inherited from their evolutionary history is the false eye pattern on their eyelids, which is fully visible when their eyes are closed. This was likely useful for making predators think they were awake while they slept to decrease the chances of attacks at night. Ettorans do not require much sleep, but they were historically weak during such times, which made these false eyes a necessary feature.
