### Culture

The Klenfahli are often compared to the [[Boullare]], since they share some physical traits and live among the trees. Otherwise, the two share very few similarities. Another society left in the wake of the [[Final Systems]], they eagerly joined the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]] upon its formation. This brought them not only modernization, but expansion into the galaxy, which brought further education beyond what their forested home had previously allowed.

Once talented hunters, the Klenfahli relied on their senses and instincts to survive. Now, they lean largely on technology, which they have contributed to in areas like weaponry and entertainment. The more traditional among them, however, still value the "first hunt," a rite of passage meant to signify when a child is ready to feed itself, and eventually a mate.

Despite their modern infatuation with technology, one thing most people of [[Klenfahl]] share is a love for nature. The planet is densely forested, and as such, it is a prime target for wood and paper production. Many people are against such practices, while some see it as merely a necessary evil, or even a job providing industry. Whatever one's outlook, it does have a negative impact on Klenfahli communities, as they do live in trees and create towns suspended from branches and limbs.

### Biology

Covered in green fur to fit with their leafy backdrop, the Klenfahli are fierce predators in their own territory. Not only are they strong climbers, but their claws and canine teeth make them naturals for catching and killing prey. Their senses are also especially keen, making them quite adept at tracking. Their prehensile tails contain thin muscles which are closely connected to the brain, and must be trained to "know" how to function, as if the brain were learning a language. If a Klenfahli grows up not knowing how to use their tail, it is extremely difficult for them to learn, though it is possible.
