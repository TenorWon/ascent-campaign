### Culture

The Gilamar are one of [[Larentum]]'s best references for evolutionary diversity, and that has contributed directly to their culture. They themselves have been responsible for much of what we now know about biology and the general sciences, urged on by the enormous pool of physical traits within their own bloodlines.

There was, of course, a time when the largest or most able Gilamar ruled over all. After all, they began as tribes amongst those with similar traits, thinking the others abominations. After almost a decade of ancient civil war, the tribes eventually signed a treaty to coexist on separate land. This coexistence would eventually see them coming together in a period known on [[Gilam]] as "[[The Enlightenment]]," due to the boom in shared knowledge and cultural exchange.

From this point on, the Gilamar thrived on knowledge. More things to discover came with technological advancement, as well as the expansion of the gene pool on Gilam. When their scientists eventually sought life beyond the stars, they were far more ready than most others. Now value is no longer determined by strength, but by intelligence one has, or by how much knowledge something can bring to an individual.

### Biology

The Gila gene pool is extremely broad, a likely reason the Gilamar were such versatile predators on their planet. From wings, to spines, to tails, to hoods, there are many variations on specialized survival traits. Most people, however, tend to possess slotted pupils, claws of varying size, forked tongues, and scaly skin. Scales come in a variety of colors, and have intermittent regenerative qualities that can make the skin difficult to pierce. Compared to other sentient species, Gilamar are quite a bit taller on average, and stronger as well.
