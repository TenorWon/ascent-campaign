### The Incursion

For millennia, the capabilities of a person stopped at what effort one devoted to one's own mind and body. However, in 5319 PET, on the 13th day of the 5th standard month of the galactic year, that changed forever when events of mass chaos and destruction broke out in most all major cities across every highly populated planet in [[Larentum]]. Authorities responded to what they thought were terrorist attacks, engaging threats in offices, schools, and city streets. What they found, however, were not terrorists.

At every site, what first responders expected to be terrorists turned out to simply be one or more civilians in states of great panic. The conditions surrounding each person varied greatly—from swirling cyclones, to raging wildfires, to massive blackouts. Many of these situations did result in casualties, including the perpetrators in some cases. However for most of the events, authorities were able to calm the civilians at the center of the supernatural conditions, which seemed to dissipate in kind.

Upon escort to medical facilities, a correlation was established between the violent, destructive conditions and the cortisol levels of the civilians involved. Doctors took great care to keep patients calm as they performed further examinations on those who had been talked down and brought in. The findings from these exams assisted authorities and medical staff in dealing with other incidents over the course of the day, as well as others that continued throughout the week.

### Modern Understanding

After nearly a millennia of time to research the Gift phenomenon, scientists have a much better understanding, though there are still plenty of questions to be answered. The first hypothetical correlation between panic and an increase in ability expression was expanded as it became understood that every emotion had an effect on a subject's Gift.

The emotional correlation theory naturally led anatomists to study the brain, which was a very fruitful endeavor. For Gifted individuals, brain activity in the emotional center is not only slightly higher, but fluctuates according to whether or not a Gift is being expressed. A great outburst of emotion, or conversely a great internalization of it, is liable to cause a Gifted ability to reveal itself. The conclusion has ultimately been drawn that Gifted individuals are born with some sort of heightened "brain potential," though that remains a theory despite the centuries of research.

Even now that it has become so much easier to identify them, Gifted are quite rare. One contributor to this is the fact that Gifts are strictly not hereditary. In fact, people with Gifts very rarely have Gifted children, as it almost always seems to skip at least one generation where applicable. However, the population of Gifted has remained steady for centuries, never seeming to dwindle even as parents with the condition have continued to have perfectly average children. Additionally, it has been found that those possessing Gifts are not born with them, as the abilities tend to first present themselves at a point during the adolescent years of a given species, and never a moment earlier. This is likely due to the increase in emotional hormone activity during such a time.

How a person's specific ability is decided is still unknown. A variety of Gifts have been recorded after much time and research. Those that have been documented are as follows:

- Control over heat
- Control over senses (one's own, and those of others)
- Control over water
- Control over light waves and particles
- Control over air and wind
- Control over sound waves
- Control over shape (one's own, and that of surrounding objects and organisms)
- Control over plant life
- Control over the minds of others, as well as greater control over one's own
- Control over biological life processes and the overall cycle of life
- Control over darkness
- Control over electricity
- Control over ground minerals, terrain, and magnetic material
- Control over deadly toxins
- Control over lesser animal life
- Control over the flow of energies, namely from one organism to another

Every Gift has proven capable of being expressed in a variety of different ways depending on the person. The reason for this is not yet known, but different regions of the galaxy do teach different strategies for Gifted ability use under their own theories. [[The Kingdom of the Sun]] and the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]] are the two biggest examples of this, with one tending to teach Gifted citizens to use a more restrained and restricted manner of expression, while the other encourages open and impassioned approach, respectively.

Different metals have been understood to react differently to contact with a Gifted individual. [[Emparite]], once thought to be simply a strong metal for construction, has now been adapted into [[veingear]] and other forms in order to take advantage of its ability to channel Gifted energy. [[Pallorite]], a rarer material only discovered after the Incursion, is used more commonly in sensory deprivation chambers, cufflinks, and other manners of restricting Gifted abilities.

### Gifted in Society

People throughout the galaxy have generally taken to Gifted quite well. As the galaxy's understanding of the Gifted condition has improved, it has quickly found ways to integrate them into society. The Kingdom of the Sun was the first, aligning the slew of supernatural skills with the needs in their workforce, then taking it a step further by creating a new step in their education pipeline just for their Gifted citizens. The Galactic Union was more than happy to welcome their Gifted, and unlike their sibling nation, they did almost nothing to inhibit or control their movements in society.

Gifted people might be rare to come across, but the galaxy's high population makes it more likely to encounter one than one might think, especially on a more populated world. Some seek to harness their control of worldly concepts, while others are happy to just exist alongside them. As empowered as they may be, they are still just people.
