### Culture

There are differing opinions when it comes to what essentially sums up the Abrilosan identity. Some would have you believe that they are the eager hosts, proudly sharing their traditions with a smile. Others would tell you that they are disdainful creatures who despise visitors, touting their culture only as a means of warding off those that they should be welcoming. Perhaps the Abrilosas themselves might tell a different story.

Despite one's outlook, all would agree that the people of [[Abrilos]] have taken quite the step back in their control of the system since the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]] established its capital there. Where they once maintained full sovereignty over their home planet, they now are only reserved certain zones of land to maintain and live on themselves. They are welcome among the many members of the large metropolitan cities that have been built on their planet, of course, but many do prefer to keep to their own, especially now that their population has decreased so much on Abrilos.

The Abrilosas are an incredibly smart people who historically relied on their intellect to survive, which historians commonly misconceive as their instincts. Although their tangential brains do provide heightened senses, it is an increased processing of neural data that allows an Abrilosa to move so efficiently. Thus it was never the strongest who was most respected, but the most intelligent, often signified by their larger horns and beaded jewelry.

### Biology

The most unique trait of the Abrilosa is their tangential brain, which is most notable for being physically exposed to open air. Of course, this is only a mortal issue in the first year of life, as the brain begins to calcify and create its own hardened shell around it, often referred to as the Abrilosa's horns. These horns are extremely hard to break after just a few years of calcification, but it is not impossible to do. The multi-directional expansion of the brain allows for faster sensory processing from the peripheral nervous system, as well as near-instant sensory processing for any information collected at points of contact with the horns, of which an individual can have 4 to 6 on average. Additionally, Abrilosas show far less need for sleep on average than other species, which is thought to be because of their unique brain structure.
