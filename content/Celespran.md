### Culture

The people of [[Celespra]] communicate with each other primarily via mood reading, as they live primarily underwater. They have created cities from caves beneath the oceans on their world, which contains very sparse patches of land and thus barely any resources above water. Celesprans had to learn quickly to navigate and defend themselves from the ocean and its creatures, and so over time they became quite the survivalists. This mindset made swathes of the people quite wary, but some became very industrious.

When the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]] welcomed their nation, the Celesprans jumped at the chance to join the fastest growing organization of governments. Until scientists got to take a look at their anatomy for the first time, no one truly understood how so many shot up the ladders of authority so quickly, but by the time the public became aware, it was already too late. It took a small-scale overhaul of diversity protocols by the [[Union Senate]] to ensure that the entire coalition was not overtaken by one interest group, which thankfully did not happen.

Celesprans are of course known for their abilities, but they are not hated for them. They are sometimes the objects of suspicion, but in a galaxy where most people are taken at their word, these expectations still apply to people who can tell how you feel at a given moment and act on it.

### Biology

This amphibious species of thick-scaled people hail from an oceanic planet that easily explains every evolutionary choice. Most notable among these is their emotional receptor gland, located just along the back of the spine. This was originally intended to allow Celesprans to communicate with each other underwater, as they were an entirely aquatic species before they developed the ability to breathe air and created the actual Celespran language. They now make use of this gland to sense the emotions of whatever living thing they choose, though this does not allow for communication with others as it would for those of their own kind.
