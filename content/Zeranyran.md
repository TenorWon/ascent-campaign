### Culture

Above all, Zeranyrans value knowledge, but not in the same way that the [[Gilamar]] do. While the Gilamar value knowledge as a means to add to their intelligence, the Zeranyrans do so as a way to seek out thrill and excitement. Finding and gaining new knowledge is thrilling to them, and thus they do so however they can. For many, this means taking to the stars. This is the natural conclusion for this people, which is theorized to have been the first species to develop any sort of space travel.

At birth, Zeranyran children are traditionally given injections into their brain. These injections contain a microchip that has been carefully designed over millennia of Zeranyran research. This microchip is meant to enhance the intelligence of the child, and grows with the individual as they age. The adaptive metal inside their brain is energized by the electricity from the synapses, allowing the functionality of the device to ramp up as the Zeranyran approaches the appropriate age to seek out their own intellectual thrill.

With their love for adventure also came a love for pioneering ideas, at least for some individuals. Thus, it is this species that ultimately gets the credit for the formation of the the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]]. When a group of systems left in disarray by the fall of the [[Final Systems]] craved unity, it was the Zeranyrans who jumped at the chance to locate a home planet to serve as the capital for their coalition.

### Biology

Zeranyrans are quite intelligent on their own, however with how closely their microchip implants are integrated into their process for newborn children, it may as well be included in their biology. The chip implants itself on the hippocampus, enhancing memory immediately upon binding to the neural matter. As the individual ages, so too does the chip expand and evolve. It can learn to enhance one's senses, their coordination, and other neurological skills. Another unique feature to Zeranyrans is that they have four eyes, though the two on each side can not move independently.
