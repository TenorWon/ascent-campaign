### Culture

Making do in the harsh conditions of the desert planet [[Velastos]], the Velastan people have endured for millennia in the hot sun. This has not been without hardship, however. Though they were able to survive the gauntlet of evolution on a planet that is still near-inhospitable, they have struggled to survive each other. Because of their tribal beginnings, these people have long stuck only to small groups of familiar individuals. When they began to expand their civilizations into larger towns and cities, they largely kept this separation.

Though there are many remaining Velastan cultural groups, there are two that are presently the largest by far and most powerful. They are called the [[Velar]] and the [[Granu]], and they went to war with each other in the year 5807 PET. The primary reasoning, according to representing [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]] senators from both groups, is that they each claim to have ancestral right to Velastos as a whole. What the smaller demographics of Velastans have to say about this is not widely known, as only the two larger groups have voices in the [[Union Senate]].

The civil war on Velastos rages on to this day, and in an attempt to impartially and diplomatically defuse the conflict, the Galactic Union has not intervened with any form of military force. Some wonder if this is due to a fear that the Velastans might secede or retaliate, or if it is simply due to the public perception that the horn-headed desert dwellers are naturally angry.

### Biology

Their hot environment has prepared the Velastan biology for dehydration and sun exposure. While their internal organs prevent exhaustion, their rosy skin tone wards off burns from the dangerous ultraviolet rays of the sun. Researchers say their horns were much larger when they walked on four legs, but evolution rendered them essentially useless as the species grew and gained sentience over millennia. That isn't to say that they don't pose any threat at all, though.
