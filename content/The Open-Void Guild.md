### Formation

The OVG has held onto its acronym since the the [[The Before Times|Before Times]], only then it was known as the Open Void Gang. Enabled by the loose grip of the [[Final Systems]], the gang was practically free to roam [[Larentum]], inhibited only by its poor organization and whatever the [[The Kingdom of the Sun|Kingdom of the Sun]] could do to fight off the crime it brought. That quickly changed, however, when the [[The War for Liberation|War for Liberation]] ended.

The Kingdom was powerful enough on its own, but with its rise as the most powerful intergalactic state came the advent of a new era of peace throughout the galaxy. This peace wasn't just coincidental with the Final Systems' absence—it was enforced by the powers that remained. Even the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]], the new coalition of recovering governments assisted by the Kingdom of the Sun, was pushing down every criminal element. If the OVG was to survive, they would need to disappear under the radar, but with a very limited amount of exposure. And disappear they did.

### Customs

The Open-Void Guild of today is a multi-layered scheme of freelancing specialists, all paid by handlers, who are paid by OVG operators further up the chain. The entire system is designed so that any layer as well as those beneath it can be cut off almost immediately without crippling the rest of the organization. Of course, the Kingdom and the Union are both well aware of the Guild's existence, which is why it would be devastating to have a freelancing agent be caught and to have that lead back all the way to the top. This way, law enforcement may get the assigned handler at most.

Due to their incredible secrecy, not much is known about the OVG, but some do know how to contact them. Finding a mercenary or smuggler on the job is a good way to get in touch by way of their handler. Normally, handlers only deal with the people who do the jobs, however. They do not appear to have contact with the people requesting the job, nor do they seem to meet in person with their superiors in the Guild. They operate as couriers of information, payment, and supplies.

One might assume that members of the OVG are aligned due to necessity and secrecy, but that is absolutely not true. As mentioned above, the only fear for the Guild is that one of their members or hired guns may have information leading back to their larger operation, but countless precautions are normally taken to prevent this before it is even possible. As a result, it is not uncommon for freelancers and their handlers to be seen competing for the same mark. So long as the job is done, the commission is shared, and business continues in the era of "peace" that seems to finally be breaking upon the shores of tension.
