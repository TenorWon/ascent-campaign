### Culture

In the long period of peace since the [[Final Systems]] fell, the powers that be have done a good job at eliminating the power that stereotypes have over a person's reputation. However, the Reshi culture is far too notorious for the galaxy to forget. The people of [[Reshremare]] are known to be ruthless, with minds more set on status and gain than on empathy, which is extremely against status quo for the state of the galaxy in the past near 1000 years.

The Mares are so unforgiving as people because their society is even more so. Weakness is seen as exploitable, and parents will watch on as their children are harassed by others because it is seen as their just punishment for showing weakness. Either the child will grow stronger, or they will sink lower and serve those at the top. This practice continues through all ages, with adult Mares critiquing their peers in scathing fashion to test their mettle. Such traditions are archaic to the rest of the galaxy, most of which have spent most of the past millennium seeing struggles for power as petty and without reward.

The political scheme on Reshremare is deeply complex. There is a central house, similar to a monarchy, which sends representatives to the [[The Galactic Union|Galactic Union]]. Of course, there are several other houses constantly fighting a complicated political (and at times physical) battle to become the central house. Such status brings control over land, title, military, and much more, but there is a long ladder for a Mare to climb in order to get to a position within such a house.

### Biology

The Mares are said to have evolved from a cave-dwelling hunter, which is why some believe that their varying shades of gray skin tones implies a sort of comfort with the darkness. This might also explain their enhanced eyesight, which is actually able to see a wider range of light wavelengths than any other sentient species in the galaxy. Scientists are still trying to see for themselves the colors that only the Mares have names for. Other than these remarkable features, they also possess a sixth finger and toe on each hand and foot, rather than the average five.
