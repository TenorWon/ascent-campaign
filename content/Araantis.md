### Culture

Like many species in the [[Larentum]] galaxy, the Araantis' culture has its roots in the physical characteristics of the people themselves. The Araantis are unlike any other species in the galaxy, demonstrating insectoid qualities in both physicality and socialization. When they rose as the primary sentients on [[Araantem]], they organized themselves quite naturally not by class, but by physical specialty. Similar to the [[Gilamar]], their physical characteristics were extremely varied, and so they assigned themselves or each other places that matched best to their abilities.

Cultural exchange with the rest of the galaxy quickly changed this, however. As other species began to show the Araantis how they governed their own people, they redirected to a more merit-based way of thinking. This did not completely destroy the notion that only certain people could do certain jobs, but as the centuries went on, this became a more traditional view and was left somewhat in the past.

A famously careful people, the Araantis tend to stick to their own if they can, especially among the older and more traditional of the people. They have fully embraced intergalactic travel and diplomacy, but in major cities across the galaxy it is not entirely uncommon to find an Araanti district. Some of the more curious or daring among the species may try to go it alone or leave their cultural environment further behind, but such choices may involve being looked down on by one's peers.

### Biology

Sentience in insectoid species is rare, but the Araantis anatomy showcases well how they earned it evolutionarily. They have claws, mandibles, compound eyes, and sensitive antennae, all of varying sizes and shapes. Beyond that, they can be born with a multitude of other features which used to enhance their survivability, and later on their place in society. These range anywhere from stingers, to wings, to specialized feet and hands for walking across different surfaces.
