### History

Drones have been adopted galaxy-wide, both as tools and even as the rare companion. Such immense advancement in technology took more than a couple centuries to properly regulate, but the galaxy has long been in a place of comfort now with drones. People allow them to teach their children, to fly them to work, to build their homes, and more.

While not responsible for the creation of drones, [[Eternus Galactic]], the galaxy's largest and most well known corporation, is known as the most reliable manufacturer for drones anywhere.

The rogue drone is few and far between, but it does happen, especially with faulty construction or tampering. These drones will often experience disconnection from their AI regulation software, throwing them into the chaos of a newly awakening consciousness.

### Class A

The responsibilities of a Class A drone normally fall under the description of "manual labor." Whether it's a rolling mail drone, a heavy loader drone, or a strong construction drone, they do a great many things that many people would rather not do anymore unless they need the work. These sorts of drones can come in all shapes and sizes, depending on what the job demands.

### Class B

More advanced the Class A's, the Class B drone is built for more intellectual tasks like piloting, teaching, and medicine. Usual built to mirror the two-legged sentient species physicality, they make use of any number of attached implements to complete their tasks safely and carefully. While Class A drones do possess some personality, these models are likely to express far more.

### Class C

The logic centers of the Class C drone are far more advanced than either of its counterparts, making it technically superior. This makes it the only machine fit to carry out combat and military tasks. These models are often built with attached weapons and implements, however they are normally programmed to handle loose weapons with some degree of skill. Not only are their mechanical brains faster, but their mechanical bodies are stronger and faster than even some organic soldiers.
