### Culture

When the people of [[Eklopani]] tasked the [[Zeranyran|Zeranyrans]] with finding a suitable capital planet for their coalition of galactic governments, it put them at the center of the development of the galaxy. Despite not being able to breathe most air aside from that of their home world, they have taken challenges in stride in order to try and establish a framework for the betterment of the systems left in ruin by the [[Final Systems]].

Eklopanites can't get around very far or fast without a drone prosthesis, which they pioneered themselves. This has put them on the forefront of much of the research and development for drone technology. It has also inspired them to dip into different areas like medicine, disability care, and organic prosthetics. They are known not just for intelligence, but also for selflessness, an example which has helped to shape the galaxy into what it is today.

This species is not simply intelligent because they live far longer on average than the average person, although they absolutely do. They are constantly testing each other and growing because of it. Most interestingly of all is that in Ekku culture, it is seen as incredibly shortsighted to feel shame at the thought of being wrong. This instinct is normally trained out of young Eklopanites quite early so that whenever they are wrong, rather than respond emotionally, they choose to admit they are wrong in order to pursue understanding.

### Biology

Eklopanites are small, gelatinous creatures that are no more than a foot around on average. Their ocular organs, or eyes, float freely through their form, though they have the ability to reset them to the points at which they are normally set, which are the upper left and right areas. They do not have mouths, but rather they breathe by taking in air molecules through the surface of their bodies and diffusing it back out. To talk, they squeeze their ocular organs like muscles, which allows them to send different sounds rippling through their viscous bodies. As for their brains, their entire bodies are technically their brains, making them quite fragile, hence their reliance on prosthetics.
