### Culture

[[The Kingdom of the Sun]]'s most surprising ally, the Borastians want for nothing more than to be what they already are. They are soldiers, bounty hunters, even pit fighters, where such a thing is possible. Of course, this is but one impression of a multidimensional species, but it is true that many Borastians grow up knowing little more than the thrill of the hunt. Their intimidating physical presence does them little favors, and Borastian elders don't seem keen on changing anyone's perception.

In their relations with the Kingdom, the people of [[Borastus]] have not often given thought to the [[Auvacianism|Auvacian]] faith, but do tend to remain amicable around the topic in order to keep things civil. Their loyalty to the [[Sunbound Monarch]] has never waivered, so the people of the galaxy have often called into question what they get out of the relationship other than being the spiky royal guardians.

As is to be expected, nonviolence is not tolerated whatsoever among the Borastians. Not only is it considered grounds for ostracization if one is not willing to kill, but one must be willing to kill their peers and enemies alike if they wish to advance in society. Borastian respect, at least in the traditional sense, is commanded by physical strength and by killing, honorable or not. Thus they are often seen in "packs" which may experience some infighting, but will at least not need to worry about if someone has the stomach to put a target down.

### Biology

The Borastians' leathery scales are thick enough to resist a great many wounds, but what made them the apex predator of their planet were the spines that covered their heads and arms. Alongside their claws and outer fangs, these people are sharp from head to toe, which made reliance on a weapon an afterthought for much of their history. When they did get their hands on weapons though, they became obsessed, quickly becoming some of the most proficient in the galaxy to ever use them.
