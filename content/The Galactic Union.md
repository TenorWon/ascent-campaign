### Formation

As the shadow of the [[The War for Liberation|War for Liberation]] regressed from over the galaxy, the potential for an age of anarchy loomed in its place. Without the [[Final Systems]], several planets were left in complete disarray. Most attempted to start their own central governments, but these authorities only had what small amounts of credibility the [[The Kingdom of the Sun|Kingdom of the Sun]] could provide in tandem with their reconstruction efforts on these worlds.

Enter the [[Eklopanite|Eklopanites]]. This highly intellectual species had not formally joined the Kingdom or any other multi-system alliance, but in the years of rebuilding that followed the war they saw opportunity. In 4828 PET, the Ekku government commissioned a [[Zeranyran]] expedition to locate a suitable world for the fragile governments of the broken worlds to unite. By chance, they happened upon [[Abrilos]], an isolated system that had yet to make any contact with [[Larentum#Galactization|galactized]] society, and were overcome with wonder. Diplomatic channels were established quickly, and the expedition fleet soon realized that it had found its destination. In the same standard year that they had left, they had found a place to unite the newly independent worlds of [[Larentum]].

In 4830, the Galactic Union was officially established. Its founders, the Eklopanites and the Zeranyrans, were joined first by the war torn [[Velastan|Velastans]], the vulnerable [[Porami]], and the listless [[Klenfahli]], with the [[Abrilosa|Abrilosas]] as members by default. Many more systems would flock over time to the banner of this new organization of unified governments, which made the Union the center for what was considered "modern" as the galaxy approached the turn of the 5th standard millennium.

In spite of how grand the Union is today, it required a great deal of assistance to remain upright, especially financially. Much of this assistance came from the [[Sunbound Monarch|Sunbound Monarchy]], who saw this new restructuring as a great way to support systems affected by the war. A lot of monetary support for the Galactic Union's government also came from well-to-do businesspeople and established corporate entities of the time. As the Union began to eventually stand on its own feet, the Kingdom of the Sun became its greatest ally, and the businesses that contributed to its rise began to integrate further into its booming economy.

### Customs

The Galactic Union was established with the goal of serving a great number of systems and peoples. That means that regardless of race, color, creed, religion, shape, size, or any other possible differentiating factor, the [[Union Senate]] shows no favoritism or discrimination. Each world is allowed three senators, and each one takes a vow to serve not only their people, but the Union as a whole. Perhaps most responsible for this is the Head Speaker, who maintains order during senate sessions and acts as a moderator. The Head Speaker is as close to a central authority figure as the Union has, as the Senate serves as the primary governing body.

As a sort of haven for those who want to make something of themselves, the Galactic Union has become a place where things become "new." There are, of course, traditional cultural groups represented all over Union worlds, but the core worlds, namely Abrilos, [[Zeranyr]], and [[New Stalvrun]] are some of of the most modernized and accepting places. [[Auvacianism|Auvacian]] practice is gladly accepted, as well as the practice of most any of the galaxy's other smaller religions. Additionally, populations tend to be more diverse, and technology more widely embraced.

Theirs being a newer society, the Union's population tends to vary far more socioeconomically. Most economists and sociologists theorize that this is simply due to the fact that they haven't had the multiple millennia that the Kingdom of the Sun has had to balance out their economy and governmental oversight. Not only that, but so many differing viewpoints and populations are likely far more difficult to optimize for the good of the people. Many businesses both native and outside of the Union contribute much to employment efforts, but pay amount fluctuates so much as the demand for labor decreases that the Senate has found it incredibly difficult to maintain consistency.

When [[Gifted]] people began to introduce themselves into society, this disrupted the economy even more. An already unstable job market was thrown into chaos by a completely insurmountable separation of skills between Gifted and non-Gifted workers. [[Credit]] scarcity was made somehow worse by the almost immediate industrial race between corporations for Gift-related or enhanced technology. Despite this, the Union Senate maintains its own economic independence in the centuries since its founding, and is doing its best to alleviate the financial burden on its people.
